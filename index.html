About this dashboard & Accounting Methodology

  • Net Savings Formula: Calculated as Net Savings = Projected All-KSEB Bill − (Actual KSEB Bill − Utility Revenue) − Solar Bill. Utility revenue earned directly offsets grid utility billing and enhances net savings.
  • Solar Fleet: South Block Solar (15 kWp), Auditorium Solar (100 kWp), and Administration Block Solar (120 kWp, commissioned April 2026).
  • KSEB Grid Interaction: High Tension HT-IIA Educational Tariff with 3 time-of-day tariff zones: Normal (6 AM–6 PM), Peak (6 PM–10 PM), and Off-peak (10 PM–6 AM), plus Maximum Demand (MD) charges.
  • Environmental Baseline: Avoided emissions calculated using the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Indian Power Sector Grid Factor (0.71 kg CO₂/kWh).
